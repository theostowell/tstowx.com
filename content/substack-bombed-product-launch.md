I’m just starting a new launch campaign for PARAZETTEL at the moment…

It’s the last one before I go relatively quiet to finish writing the book around note-taking that I announced and pre-sold to my subscribers back around Christmas time.

Right now, even though it’s very early in the pipeline, I’m feeling a little uncertainty as to how the campaign is going to perform. This doesn’t matter though, because I’ve found success in the space I occupy in the world of ‘Info’ is just a matter of throwing shit at the wall and seeing what sticks.

I launched my first info product (if you can call it that) around 3 years ago. It was a three-document series about how to grow on Medium, the platform I describe to people as the YouTube of writing.

Having done what I thought was relatively well in my first four or five months posting there, I was excited to share to people what I’d learned.

I had perhaps seven people download that product, period.

But it wasn’t six months later when I launched an Obsidian template to an email list I’d spent the summer growing that I cracked the code, and made over a grand in a weekend, with next-to-zero experience prior to this success project.

*(It still haunts me how under-priced this template was, as well. But that’s a lesson learned)*

Since then, I’ve been running the same template offer, updating and improving it, to the extent that it’s a part of the offer I’m sharing in this new campaign. If it’s not broke, don’t fix it, as they say.

And since that successful template, perhaps only 1 of 5 attempted new offers have really stuck, and the winner (the PARAZETTEL Community Premium membership) joins the template in this final bundle before book publish, creating one big chunk of knowledge, for one price.

In essence, it’s that I’ve dedicated my free time to building over the last three years.

The reason I’m doing this now?

At this point in my life, having finished studying and travel (at least for now), I’ve got a great chance to take stock of my business and my wider life, choosing where to take it in the future. I could continue running the same Info offers, as they still work (or I assume that they do, I’ll update you in a short fortnight), but that doesn’t excite me.

The book I’m publishing opens me up into a wider set of options in the field of note-taking and information management, and I want to put all of my new business understanding to the test, and see if I can put together something better and more personal, something high-ticket (that does a better job of financing a life as someone who travels and works).

And that’s not to mention the power of AI that exists now, so that I can build custom platforms, templates and tools that I couldn’t back when I was building the older offers.

In such a time of opportunity, it’s time for something more. And that starts with rounding things out nicely for this era, with the current bundle offer, and then publishing my first book!
