There's been more than a short period of relative radio silence from me recently, especially for someone who's made a name for writing content on the internet.

Luckily I've not given up, and there's a reason for the time that I've spent off. In short, I've been writing a book!

![[Helium 2026-09-25 15.43.55.png]]

It's for the PARAZETTEL business, and it's called Minimal Note-Taking, and it's all about how to simplify the process of choosing how to work with your personal knowledge, based on goals and priorities, rather than what the best internet marketing teams want to force on you.

This project took longer and was harder to complete than I first anticipated, for a number of reasons. It feels as though my focus has decayed, from all the context switching, but to finally get the release over the line was very 

It's been well over a year since I wrote something even a quarter of the length of this book, so the skills were a little rusty. However, it reminded me that in this world of getting agents to do jobs, and becoming a 'meat proxy', as the tech and specifically programming industry has coined, the power of being able to sit down and ideate for a long stretch, as well as write, read, and edit, is not something that we should easily let go of.

I've still not read any fully AI-generated text that was worth its salt, either from a uniquely informational or creative perspective, and that makes sense - anything that these models come up with is sythesised to be the non-committal middle of what the world thinks the solution is.

The real insight is found in the fringes, and in the fringes we still need true masters of craft, teaching because of the value in human-to-human transmission. I don't deem myself to be there right now, with this first edition of a book about a principle that's evergreen, but will continue to be developed and shaped over the course of my life.

I hope to have it on Amazon one day, not competing but sitting alongside the works of those who've inspired me to synthesise and share my understanding of the world and more specifically note-taking.

So that's the book. If you want to buy it, you can go here - https://parazettel.com/book. Otherwise, now that this project is at a close for the foreseeable future, you're going to hear a lot more from me from a content perspective, because I've realised that great writing and video and creating it is something that really matters to me from a fulfilment perspective, so alongside the book I'm building the PARAZETTEL business to be something that's largely automated once emails have been collected - aside from this, I just have to write articles, record videos and every day build information and teaching, adn share it with the world. I have other slightly more ambitious projects as well, but building up a good grounds of online audience is going to help with this as well. keep yourselves tuned!
