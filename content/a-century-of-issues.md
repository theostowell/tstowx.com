It’s been a long time since the fateful day at the end of May 2023 when I decided to publish my first newsletter issue.

And since this, much has happened and Fundamentalised has changed substantially. Since I explain much of this in the video, I’m going to share a shorter roundup…

- [I’ve got a one-way plane ticket to Bangkok, Thailand](https://www.fundamentalised.com/p/095-a-smart-business-and-character?utm_source=tstowx&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=100-a-century-of-issues-whats-next), with plans to travel the southeast of Asia whilst growing [my business PARAZETTEL](https://go.parazettel.com/?utm_source=tstowx&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=100-a-century-of-issues-whats-next).
- Fundamentalised has been text-first up until now, but the adventures I’ll be having will call for videos, so I’m publishing vlogs and other clips here on Substack as well.
- Follow me on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/theostowell?utm_source=tstowx&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=100-a-century-of-issues-whats-next) and [Twitter](https://x.com/theostowell?utm_source=tstowx&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=100-a-century-of-issues-whats-next) for shorter content as I make an attempt to harmonise all my personal channels into places showing my progress on growing PARAZETTEL and shooting photo and video as a freelancer.
- We might publish a little less than weekly, but likely much more. There’s more adventures and experiences to have, but the internet and consistency is a lot patchier. I’ll share when I get the opportunity.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart to you for being here to read and watch. I have no idea how the future’s going to pan out, but the fact that I can even have a punt at travelling, working and sharing what I’m doing is what dreams are made of.

See you next week, from Thailand!

— Theo
