In an ideal world, I just wake up and create what calls to me. 

Although right now I earn money and feel good creating the content that I do create, it doesn't particularly call to me in the way that these essays do, that the short takes that i sometimes come up with but never post do.

i don't like the management and commitment side of things. managing all the different social accounts, worrying if i'm engaging enough on them and making sure that the followers are ahppy.

but at the same time I know that at least for know it's a necessary evil, seeing as I'd rather do what I'm doing at the moment than work for some other cause than my own doing something I dislike.

The things that I want to create vary - sometimes writing is what I want to do, and sometime photography really calls to me.
