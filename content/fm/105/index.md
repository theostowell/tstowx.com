I’m on my way to Da Nang, Vietnam in a little more than 24 hours.

However, it’s been a month since I did a video issue, so this is a longer, round-up-style post to talk you through what I’ve done in the last month.

In short…

- I started in Bangkok and got tired of the chaos and parties very quickly, and then…

- Moved to Krabi, to spend some time by the sea, and got similar tied up in relaxing and partying, but then…

- I properly needed a break to get on with some work, so went to Chiang Mai for a week. It was great, with good coffee and gym, and I met some great people, and then…

- I headed to Koh Phangan to see off two friends who I travelled through Bangkok and Krabi with, as they both had to head home to Europe, and then…

- I came back to Bangkok, this time living in the Sukhumvit area, in a much more sustainable way.

And that’s where I am right now. In the rest of the video I discuss some of my plans for Vietnam, as well as which place I’m most likely to settle down in out of everywhere I’ve been so far.

Enjoy, and I’ll see you soon from a new place,

— Theo
