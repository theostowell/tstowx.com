A little update from my hotel room in Da Nang. Since Hoi An went underwater I returned here and lived in a hostel for a week before my friend Brook arrived in the city.

Now we’ve got adjoining rooms and are focusing on work. He’s got a website due for a client tomorrow, and I’ve just got to make money to continue living out here.

In a fortnight I’m going to China to meet a friend and former university housemate of mine, which will be great, but for now I’ve got to focus a bit.

Thanks for standing by! I’m still posting images from my travels [over on Instagram](https://instagram.com/tstowx), where the most recent of which are images from the floods in Hoi An.

See you soon for another update!
