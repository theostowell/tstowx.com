Recently I've been battling the administrative tasks behind posting and sharing ideas online, as well as online business. 

After I launched the PARAZETTEL Community a few weeks back, aside from creating one more course which I [launched the past weekend](https://skool.com/parazettel), I feel as though I've just been poking around with pointless tweaks and admin that aren't ever going to make a significant difference in the business. 

This prompted me to think about what I actually want out of going on this journey, and I realised that I just want to create what I want, every day, without being tied to a certain something or another.

As in one day I write an essay and have somewhere to publish it, and some day 

And I believe that I can get to this point, and will continue to create things that satisfy me until I reach that point, but not without some sucking it up and addressing using social media to grow an audience and to reach people worth connecting with in a business sense, because I'm going to need some way of supporting myself and I'd rather do that by creating than by working on somebody else's dream.

But I know that this could be done better, and in a way that better prioritises creating, rather than consuming.

So I've been simplifying things. I know, there's only so many times that I can go down this topic of newsletter, but it's become so relevant in this path that I've been walking recently that I thought I'd shine another light on the utility of cutting things back.

One such thing that I've been struggling with more recently is that of marketing and the admin processes behind the business, as I've mentioned. The email marketing, now that I got a second offer on the go has to be re-targeted and organised in a new way, and I'd not been getting down to work on that. So yesterday morning, I just sat down and got the whole thing done.

Now, rather than a whole host of different sequences and automations, I've got one main content sequence that everyone receives, with dynamic elements in the emails to pitch them the right kind of move to progress within the funnel.

Another thing that's been weighing on my mind recently is that of social media (surprise, surprise). Although I don't feel caught up in consumption on the platforms, they seem to be weighing on my mind a lot.
