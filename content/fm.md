Fundamentalised was the letter I sent for years before this site existed: essays, arguments with
myself, and the occasional note about whatever I was building at the time. It has changed shape more
than once, and it is now mostly folded into the writing on this site.

The back issues live here as they come across. Oldest first, no editing of the past.
