Right now I'm taking a break from writing and filming newsletters.

Here's why...



---

At the moment I'm closing in on the end of my time at university. In a few months I'll have graduated and will be out in the world doing adult things.

Now for the longest time I didn't really think past the cushy guardrails of university. They don't really care what you get up to here, so long as you pass your exams.

It's almost like they don't care whether I pass or not - oh wait - I had to remind myself that they're getting close to ten grand every year that I spend here, whether I do well or not, so they're not that fussed so long as I pass.

But out in the real world, nobody gives a damn, in short.

So I've got to prioritise my time - and for now, growing the business and finding housing and work where I want to live is where the effort should be flowing.

And although it really hurts, Fundamentalised and the creation of issues thereof doesn't really help all that much with this.

But don't worry - I'm documenting the entire journey on [Twitter](https://x.com/fundamentalised) and there will be monumental video and written issues telling the entire story the first time I come back.

So I won't be ghosting you, but things might be quieter around here. 

I hope you understand,

-- Theo
