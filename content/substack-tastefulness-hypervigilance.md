I’m so damn tired of what I see on the internet every day...

I log into Medium to post articles as marketing for my business. Then I head to substack. and then over to twitter. And the story is the same on every platform...

AI slop is completely unavoidable now.

Nobody knows how to use it right to write a piece.

And the ones who think they’ve figured it out, that people don’t notice, are wrong. There are a select group who do. Those who are tasteful and hypervigilant.

Unfortunately I lump myself into the same bucket, at least when it comes to consuming written content (i live it every day for work after all).

I can’t lose myself in a text anymore. There’s a feeling that I have to keep looking over my shoulder, has this person just outsourced their entire workflow to AI? Did they really earn my care to read, when they’ve not cared about how it looks once put out?

*This piece is moving so smoothly, there’s no friction. It’s gotta be ai-generated...*

Things like this are running through my head. A piece of writing has to stink of human now for me to be comfortable.

And that’s why I’ve stopped caring so much about how ‘well’ I write. Dare I say I was on to something in one of my ancient newsletter issues from 2023 or so...

Look at this piece. It’s objectively badly written. Like all the new lines and things, and the sentences that are a little long and awkward.

It’s written by someone who’s woken up an hour or two earlier than normal, sat on total 2 hours of bus today and frazzled their receptors with a net caffeine overdose (i.e. over my baseline of ~500mg day), at 8pm when they should be winding down, rather than a perfectly rational model who’s going to use all the logic of probability and modern computing to calculate which string of words is going to slide the smoothest and most tastlessly down the metaphorical mental gullet.

and that gives it value. and I’m perfectly aware that I’m not the first person to say these exact things I’ve taken time out of everyone’s day to say again, and I’m not always going to write this shit, but at least it’s human.

I’m tired of slop, but at the same realise that because of it, it’s never been easier to write ‘badly’ and subsequently stand out positively.

let’s see how this continues to unfold.

— Theo
