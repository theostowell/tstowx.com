# World Tour: Building a Business Through Travel

I'm currently on a journey around the world, combining my passions for photography, meaningful work, and personal growth. This page documents my adventure as I travel, create, and grow my business on the road.

## What I'm Doing

### Photography for People
I'm offering photography services as I travel—capturing portraits, events, and stories for individuals and businesses in each location I visit. Whether it's personal branding shots, travel memories, or commercial work, I'm here to document the moments that matter.

### Volunteering
To make this journey sustainable, I'm volunteering through platforms like Worldpackers and similar opportunities. This allows me to:
- Reduce living costs while traveling
- Immerse myself in local communities
- Gain unique experiences and perspectives
- Build meaningful connections around the world

### Growing the Business
While on the road, I'm actively building my business:
- Creating educational content about note-taking and productivity
- Working on [Parazettel](https://parazettel.com) and my upcoming book
- Building sustainable income streams
- Documenting the journey through photography and writing
