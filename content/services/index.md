Making use of my followers' inboxes allowed me to gauge interest in the product and what a handful of eager volunteers thought about it pre-release, before integrating their suggestions and first launching successfully in September 2023 ([read this first email marketing sequence here](https://drive.proton.me/urls/XPM3HCC5R8#NKg7ByvLvvq5)).
